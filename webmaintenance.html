Frequently Asked Questions

Website maintenance questions.

What does website maintenance include?

Website maintenance can include security updates, backups, content updates, bug fixes, performance optimization, software updates and website monitoring.

Why does a website need maintenance?

Regular website maintenance helps keep a website secure, functional, updated, fast and reliable for visitors.

Can Mutacore Visuals maintain an existing website?

Yes. Mutacore Visuals can maintain existing websites as well as websites developed by the company.

How often should a website be maintained?

The ideal maintenance schedule depends on the website, its technology and how frequently it is updated. Regular monitoring and scheduled maintenance can help prevent problems.
